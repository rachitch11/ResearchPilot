BUILD PROMPT — RESEARCHPILOT FROM SCRATCH
Build a complete production-style AI web application called ResearchPilot from scratch.
You are acting as a senior full-stack engineer, AI engineer, UI/UX designer, and product architect.
Do not build a generic chatbot. Build a real AI research product with an agentic research pipeline, web research, evidence extraction, source verification, citations, analytics, evaluation, error handling, and a polished SaaS-quality interface.
The initial application must be designed to run on Replit using free/open-source technologies and currently available free tiers.
The target is approximately ₹0/$0 for the MVP, subject to the current quotas and limitations of external free-tier APIs and Replit.
Do not require any paid service for the basic V1 functionality.

1. CORE PRODUCT
Product name:
ResearchPilot
Tagline:
AI-powered research that finds, verifies, and explains.
Purpose:
A user enters a complex research question.
Example:
Compare the EV markets of India, USA and Germany and determine which market has the strongest growth potential over the next five years.
ResearchPilot should autonomously:
    1. Understand the question.
    2. Break it into research sub-questions.
    3. Create a research plan.
    4. Generate targeted search queries.
    5. Search the web.
    6. Collect relevant sources.
    7. Classify sources.
    8. Retrieve webpage content.
    9. Extract evidence and claims.
    10. Cross-check important claims.
    11. Detect conflicting information.
    12. Synthesize the evidence.
    13. Generate a professional research report.
    14. Attach citations to important factual claims.
    15. Show high-level research progress to the user.
    16. Record anonymous usage analytics.
    17. Provide a private admin analytics dashboard.
    18. Handle failures gracefully.
    19. Provide an evaluation framework to measure quality.
